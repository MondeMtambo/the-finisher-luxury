2. South African Tax & VAT Compliance Framework

SARS Regulatory Status (VAT Exemption Advantage):
Under South African tax legislation (VAT Act No. 89 of 1991, Section 23), registration for Value-Added Tax (VAT) is only mandatory once a business generates over R1,000,000 in taxable turnover within a consecutive 12-month period.
  • Current Positioning: Mtambo Holdings (Pty) Ltd operates fully tax-compliant and pays Corporate Income Tax (27%), but does not levy 15% VAT on clients at this stage.
  • Client Benefit: South African SMEs and agencies save an immediate 15% cash outlay compared to VAT-registered corporate vendors.
  • Invoice Clause: Official invoices will state: "All amounts in South African Rand (ZAR). VAT is zero-rated / not applicable under SARS Section 23 (<R1M threshold)."

7. Glossary of Terms (Plain English Explanations)

To ensure clear communication across both executive leadership and non-technical stakeholders, the key technical and financial terms used in this proposal are explained below:

1. CRM (Customer Relationship Management)
Software that helps a business manage and track all interactions with clients, deals, sales pipelines, follow-ups, and customer service requests in one unified platform.
2. SaaS (Software-as-a-Service)
A cloud software distribution model where customers pay a recurring subscription (monthly or yearly) to access the application via a web browser, rather than buying and installing permanent software on physical computers.
3. MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)
The predictable, highly reliable revenue that Mtambo Holdings generates every single month from active subscriptions. If 50 clients pay R999/month, MRR is R49,950.
4. ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)
The annualized value of recurring subscriptions (MRR multiplied by 12 months). This is the key benchmark investors, banks, and acquirers use to evaluate the net worth of a software business.
5. Unit Economics
The direct revenues and costs associated with a single customer. It answers the fundamental question: "After paying the server, database, and email costs for one client, how much pure profit is left?" (In our case: >85%).
6. Forex Risk (Foreign Exchange Exposure)
The financial vulnerability that occurs when a South African company pays for software in US Dollars. When the Rand weakens against the Dollar, the software becomes significantly more expensive without any warning. The Finisher eliminates this by billing exclusively in fixed ZAR.
7. Per-Seat Tax (Per-User Penalty)
A pricing practice popularized by Salesforce where companies are charged a heavy fee for every single user login. The Finisher avoids this by offering generous team packs (e.g. 5 users for R999).
8. Tenant Isolation & Multi-Tenancy
An architectural security standard where every client’s data (deals, contacts, assets) is sealed inside its own private digital vault. Client A can never view or access Client B’s data under any circumstances.
9. CIPC (Companies and Intellectual Property Commission)
The official South African government agency responsible for registering and verifying companies, directors, and legal compliance. The Finisher integrates business profiles directly with registered CIPC entity records.
10. POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act)
South Africa’s primary data privacy law (Act 4 of 2013). Section 19 legally obligates companies to protect customer personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or data breaches.
11. Pro-Forma Invoice
An official preliminary bill sent to a corporate client requesting payment before service activation, complete with Mtambo Holdings bank account details, branch code, and payment reference.
12. SLA (Service Level Agreement)
A formal commitment defining uptime standards, response times, and priority support guarantees provided by Mtambo Holdings to enterprise subscribers.
